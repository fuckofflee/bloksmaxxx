LOOP

Mobile (Android or iPhone)

Phones cannot use extensions or virtual cables. Instead, a bookmark you tap on the conference page, before joining the call swaps the microphone for your audio file.

One-time setup

  1. Tap Copy bookmark code.
  2. Bookmark any page (Chrome: ⋮ → ☆. Safari: Share → Add Bookmark).
  3. Edit that bookmark: name it LOOP, replace its address with the copied code.

Each time

  1. Open the conference link (Meet, Teams, Zoom web, Jitsi...) but do not join yet.
  2. Open the bookmark LOOP (Chrome Android: type LOOP in the address bar and tap the bookmark suggestion; Safari: open Bookmarks and tap it).
  3. A black bar appears at the bottom. Tap AUDIO, choose your file(s). ⏸ / ▶ pauses and resumes. Tap ⚙ for mode (play once / loop selected / loop playlist / shuffle), gap, volume, voices (1–7, layers an echoing chorus of the same message), which microphone to replace, and "hear it locally".
  4. Join the call. The microphone is the audio file.

Reloading the page removes the bar: tap the bookmark again. Some sites block bookmarklets; if nothing appears, try another browser (Firefox, Safari, Chrome).
